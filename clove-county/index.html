About Clove County

Clove County is an iconic residential development in Sector 151, Noida, crafted by County Group — renowned creators of legendary landmarks such as Cleo County and Ivory County. Designed exclusively for discerning families and high-net-worth buyers seeking space, privacy, and refinement.

Comprising only three ultra-high-rise towers rising G+45 storeys across a 5-acre landscaped campus, the master plan allocates over 70% to pristine open gardens, water bodies, and leisure avenues. Offering grand 4 BHK and 5 BHK layouts ranging from 4,077 to 6,417 sq. ft.
