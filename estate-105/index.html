About Estate 105

Estate 105 is Max Estates' flagship luxury residential community in Delhi-NCR, conceived under the philosophy that life is best lived in motion. Designed for everyday vitality and holistic well-being, active movement is seamlessly woven into the architectural master plan, verdant landscapes, and daily routines.

Located directly on the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway in Sector 105, the estate seamlessly blends with the adjacent central green corridor. With only 4 residences per floor served by dedicated private elevators, residents enjoy absolute privacy, tranquil open spaces, and world-class sports and wellness amenities.
