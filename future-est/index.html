Project Overview

Harmonious Living Redefined at Sector 1, Greater Noida West

Future Estate is a masterfully planned residential and commercial development situated at Plot No. GH-16E, Sector-01, Greater Noida West, close to Gaur Mulberry Mansion and Balak Inter College. Spread across approximately 32,000.40 sq. meters, the project is set within an enviable low-density environment enhanced by an expansive 100-meter-wide green belt for pure, serene living.

Developed in two distinct phases, Future Estate is inspired by a unique music-themed architectural concept that embodies harmony, rhythm, and timeless elegance. The development houses 7 meticulously crafted towers comprising 1,008 residential homes alongside 50 curated commercial spaces. Built with earthquake-resistant RCC frame engineering, granite-finished kitchens, and superior fittings, Future Estate guarantees longevity, safety, and enduring value.
