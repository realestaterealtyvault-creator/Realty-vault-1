ACE ARTE is strategically situated at Plot No. SC-02/G, Sector 150, Noida, Uttar Pradesh – 201310. The sector is widely celebrated for its low-density master plan, golf-course-oriented environs, and direct connectivity across the Noida region.

The development offers exclusively expansive 3 BHK, 4 BHK, and 4.5 BHK neo-classical residences designed with expansive living spaces, high ceilings, and double-height architectural verandas.
