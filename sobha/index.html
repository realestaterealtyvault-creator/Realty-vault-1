About Sobha Rivana

Welcome to an architectural masterpiece where opulence finds its purest form. Sobha Rivana, located at prime Sector 1, Greater Noida West, is crafted for those who value world-class German-standard construction, serene green landscapes, and exquisite privacy. Spread across 12 tranquil acres, the project features eight magnificent towers rising up to 45 storeys.

With an exclusive 4-apartment-per-floor configuration complemented by dedicated private lifts, every home offers panoramic scenic vistas, expansive sundecks, and lavish cross-ventilation. Designed around an expansive 35,000 sq.ft. clubhouse and resort amenities, Sobha Rivana seamlessly links you to Central Noida, Delhi, and the upcoming Jewar International Airport.

Operating across Dubai, India, Oman, and Bahrain, Sobha has delivered millions of square feet of landmark real estate with an impeccable record of timely delivery and uncompromised build quality.
